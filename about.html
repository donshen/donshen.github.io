About

This is a personal site. I use it to write up papers properly, usually after a claim in one bothered me enough that I went and checked it.

The posts here run long, and they tend to include things you can poke at, because I do not really trust a diagram I cannot change. Where I could not verify something, I say so in the post rather than burying it in a footnote.

There is no schedule. Things appear when they are worth the time it takes to write them up.

If you want to follow along, there is a feed.
